Step-by-Step Launch Plan · Mobile Shop · Bapuji Nagar, Bhubaneswar, Khurda, Odisha

Your 6-Month Launch Roadmap — Bhubaneswar

📅 WEEK 1–2
1

Apply for PMEGP at DIC Bhubaneswar

Visit the District Industries Centre (DIC), Khurda — located near Rasulgarh, Bhubaneswar. Ask for PMEGP application for service sector. Carry: Aadhaar, bank passbook, 3 passport photos, and this analysis as your project summary. Processing takes 30–60 days but you can set up the shop in parallel.

📋 Documents: Aadhaar + Bank Passbook + Project Report 💰 Benefit: Up to ₹1.4L subsidy on ₹4L project
2

Apply for Mudra Kishore Loan at SBI Bhubaneswar

Visit SBI Bapuji Nagar Branch or Bank of India, Unit-4. Request PM Mudra Yojana – Kishore category for ₹2.5 Lakh. This is collateral-free and approved in 7–15 days. Submit simultaneously with PMEGP application. Combined, these two schemes can fund up to ₹3.9L of your ₹4L requirement.

📋 Docs: Aadhaar + PAN + 6-month bank statement 💰 Loan: Up to ₹2.5L, zero collateral
📅 WEEK 3–4
3

Find a Shop in Bapuji Nagar & Get GST + Udyam

Look for a 120–180 sq ft shop on the Bapuji Nagar Main Road or near the Bapuji Nagar market complex. Negotiate a 6-month rent lock-in. Budget ₹15,000–18,000/month for rent in this area. Register for GST at gst.gov.in — mandatory for phone distributors. Get Udyam Registration free at udyamregistration.gov.in.

🔑 GST + Udyam: Both free, online 🏪 Rent target: ₹15,000–18,000/month
📅 MONTH 1–2
4

Source Stock from Bhubaneswar Wholesale Distributors

Contact distributors in Bhubaneswar's electronics wholesale market at Kharavela Nagar and Bomikhal. Key contacts: Odisha Mobile Traders (Samsung, Realme, Redmi) and Biswa Electronics (itel, Micromax, accessories). Start with ₹1.9L in phones (30 units mixed) + ₹50K in accessories. Negotiate 15-day credit with PAN + GST proof.

📦 Stock: ₹1.9L phones + ₹50K accessories 💡 Get 15-day credit from distributors
📅 MONTH 2–3
5

Launch & Build Local Reputation in Bapuji Nagar

Distribute 500 flyers in Bapuji Nagar and nearby colonies. Share your shop launch in Bapuji Nagar colony WhatsApp groups and BJB College groups. Offer one free repair service to the first 50 customers to build word-of-mouth. Give a written 30-day guarantee on all repairs — this differentiates you from every competitor in the area.

📢 Marketing: Bapuji Nagar colony WhatsApp groups 🎁 Offer: 30-day written repair guarantee
📅 MONTH 3–6
6

Bajaj Finance EMI Point + Jio/Airtel Retailer + Premium Range

Apply to become a Bajaj Finance EMI Point — this lets customers buy ₹10K–₹30K phones on zero-cost EMI from your shop. This is a game-changer in urban Bhubaneswar. Also register as a Jio/Airtel retailer for recharge + SIM commissions (₹3,000–5,000/month extra). By Month 4, add premium phones (Samsung A-series, iPhone 12 refurbished) to target government employee customers.

💳 Bajaj Finance EMI = Higher value sales 📲 Jio Retailer = ₹4K/month extra
